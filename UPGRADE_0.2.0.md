Status: adopted

## Purpose

Thalean Mechanics 0.2.0 adds Representation Completeness as a
mechanics law.

The upgrade does not enlarge the primitive ontology of the thalion.

The thalion remains the primitive computational object.

Canonical Registration remains the objective resolved registration of
a thalion and exists independently of representation.

## Existing v0.1 ontology

Version 0.1.0 established:

- the thalion as primitive computational object;
- the canonical 60-address space;
- exactly one Canonical Registration;
- objectivity of Canonical Registration;
- observation as registration;
- representations derived through registration interpreters;
- representations as distinct from the thalion;
- bits as representations rather than primitive objects;
- computational records as preservers rather than registrations;
- context as non-redefining;
- the ontological ordering;
- minimality of the primitive ontology.

These commitments remain intact.

## Project 41 provenance boundary

Project 41 Audits 109A through 109H supplied the immediate provenance
for this mechanics upgrade.

### Audit109A

Canonical Registration was separated from older Project 41
representation-level uses of the phrase "canonical registration."

The historical registration-index-0 object was classified as an
icosahedral seam registration, not ontological Canonical Registration.

No explicit finite carrier for ontological Canonical Registration was
found in the inspected normative v0.1 sources.

### Audit109B

An abstract registration-carrier specification was introduced:

    r in R_T

with deterministic registration interpreters

    I_j : R_T -> X_j.

No finite carrier, cardinality, or inverse interpreter was assumed.

### Audit109C

Ten existing Project 41 finite objects were tested as possible
registration carriers.

None was promoted to R_T.

The candidates included G60, G30, Actor120, the three sixty-state Actor
shadows, the local V4 fiber, the 028 seam registrations, the registered
history groupoid, and r1 / Thalean time.

### Audit109D

Actor120 supplied a native example of joint faithfulness.

Each of its three sixty-state quotient shadows is individually
two-to-one.

Any two distinct shadows jointly recover the complete 120-state Actor.

### Audit109E

Actor120 was proved to be the exact pairwise compatibility object of
its three native shadows over their common G30 base.

The raw three-shadow G30 fiber product contains 240 tuples.

The lawful Actor compatibility locus contains 120.

Locally the compatibility relation is

    e xor h xor t = 0.

This is an exact finite mechanics theorem.

It does not identify Actor120 with Canonical Registration.

### Audit109F

The general joint-interpreter architecture was formalized.

For

    I_j : R_T -> X_j,

define

    Delta_I(r) = (I_j(r))_j

and

    C_I = image(Delta_I).

Joint faithfulness means Delta_I is injective.

### Audit109G

A conditional reconstruction theorem was established.

If a registration-interpreter family is jointly faithful, then

    Delta_I : R_T -> C_I

is bijective.

Thus compatible representation data can faithfully realize Canonical
Registration without identifying Canonical Registration with any
individual representation.

The theorem remained conditional because no actual ontological
registration-interpreter family had yet been constructed.

### Audit109H

The existing Thalean Mechanics doctrine was searched for a prior
representation-completeness commitment.

The searched archive contained substantial faithful and nonfaithful
representation language, but those results concerned receipt actions,
histories, projections, realizations, and other representation-specific
objects.

No existing v0.1 commitment was found requiring registration
interpreters jointly to distinguish Canonical Registrations.

Representation Completeness is therefore a new mechanics commitment,
not a rediscovered v0.1 theorem.

Axiom 10 states:

    The ontology of the thalion is minimal.

    Further ablation invalidates the thalion.

    Further definition belongs to mechanics rather than ontology.

Representation Completeness therefore belongs to mechanics.

It is not an additional constituent of the primitive thalion.

The minimality assault leaves Axioms 1 through 10 unchanged.

The thalion ontology remains version 0.1.0.

The machine-readable thalion definition remains version 0.1.0.

## Representation Completeness Principle

A canonical thalion admits a lawful family of deterministic
registration interpreters whose joint action is faithful to Canonical
Registration.

Let

    I_j : R_T -> X_j

be the lawful interpreter family.

Define

    Delta_I(r) = (I_j(r))_j.

The family is jointly faithful when

    I_j(r) = I_j(r') for every j

implies

    r = r'.

Equivalently,

    Delta_I

is injective.

## Compatibility locus

Define

    C_I = image(Delta_I).

A representation tuple is compatible exactly when it belongs to C_I.

Compatibility means common-source consistency.

It does not require distinct representations to be equal or directly
comparable.

For a jointly faithful family,

    Delta_I : R_T -> C_I

is bijective.

Thus compatible representation data uniquely determine the source
Canonical Registration.

## What version 0.2.0 changes

Thalean Mechanics 0.2.0 adds:

- Representation Completeness;
- Joint Faithfulness;
- Compatibility Locus;
- the distinction between individual and joint representational
  fidelity;
- a formal reconstruction condition for compatible representation
  families.

These are mechanics-level additions.

## What version 0.2.0 does not change

Version 0.2.0 does not change:

- the primitive status of the thalion;
- the canonical 60-address ontology;
- Canonical Registration as objective source;
- Axioms 1 through 10;
- the machine-readable thalion schema;
- the ontological ordering.

It does not claim:

- Canonical Registration is G60;
- Canonical Registration is G30;
- Canonical Registration is Actor120;
- Canonical Registration is a graph vertex;
- Canonical Registration is a bit;
- Canonical Registration is a computational record;
- Canonical Registration is registered history;
- Canonical Registration is Thalean time;
- every individual representation is faithful;
- every possible representation belongs to the lawful complete family.

## Finite motivating model

The Actor results provide the first exact finite model motivating this
mechanics law.

Actor120 has three sixty-state shadows.

No single shadow is faithful.

Any two distinct shadows are jointly faithful.

Their lawful three-way compatibility locus occupies exactly half of the
raw G30-compatible triple space.

The local law is

    e xor h xor t = 0.

This model demonstrates that Representation Completeness is
mathematically realizable.

It is not the ontological proof of the principle.

## Informative Action boundary

Informative Action remains:

    a lawful action capable of changing Canonical Registration.

Representation Completeness does not prove that Actor Enactment changes
Canonical Registration.

The ontology-to-mechanics source bridge remains open.

Actor Enactment therefore remains a mechanics operation rather than a
certified Thalean Informative Action.
