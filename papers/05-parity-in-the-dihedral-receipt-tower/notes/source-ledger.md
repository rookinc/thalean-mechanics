# Source ledger

The proof is self-contained and uses elementary finite group theory.

Paper 4 supplies historical motivation for an order-`2n` lifted successor:
a nontrivial binary receipt over `C_n` forces `C_2n`. The present theorem
begins with an order-`2n` product of two involutions and derives its complete
dihedral and parity structure independently.

Papers 2 and 3 are recovered as the special case `n=4`. They are context,
not logical premises of the general proof.

The executable audit constructs explicit permutation realizations for
`3 <= n <= 32`, enumerates every group element, and verifies every parity,
quotient, commutator, and normal-form identity. This is a finite witness for
the symbolic proof, not a restriction of the theorem to that range.
