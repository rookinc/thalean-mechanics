\begin{abstract}
We classify finite geometry generated by one reversible visible
successor together with a binary action receipt. The visible system is
a directed four-cycle. A deterministic \(C_2\)-covariant two-sheeted
lift is specified by one binary voltage on each edge, and their sum is
the circuit holonomy. Trivial holonomy produces two disjoint
four-cycles. Nontrivial holonomy produces one connected eight-cycle.
Each holonomy value determines one gauge class. Therefore a visible
four-cycle, a binary receipt fiber, and a nontrivial full-circuit
receipt uniquely force the connected \(C_8\) lift up to
fiber-preserving gauge and isomorphism.

Iteration supplies states, one-step reachability supplies incidence,
and the binary lift retains history across visible return. Four steps
restore visible phase while exchanging the receipt sheet; eight steps
are required for lifted closure. Angle assignments and physical
interpretations are outside the theorem.
\end{abstract}
