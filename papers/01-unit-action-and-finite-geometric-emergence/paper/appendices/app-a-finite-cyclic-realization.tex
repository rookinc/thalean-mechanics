\section{Finite census}

The sixteen voltage words in \(C_2^4\) form two gauge classes:
\[
(0,0,0,0)\quad\text{and}\quad(0,0,0,1).
\]

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c|c|c|c|c}
Holonomy & Words & Classes & Components & Order\\
\hline
\(0\) & \(8\) & \(1\) & \(C_4\sqcup C_4\) & \(4\)\\
\(1\) & \(8\) & \(1\) & \(C_8\) & \(8\)
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

For \((0,0,0,1)\), the orbit is
\[
(0,0),(1,0),(2,0),(3,0),
(0,1),(1,1),(2,1),(3,1),(0,0).
\]
