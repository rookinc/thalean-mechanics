\section{Visible unit action}

Let \(V=\Zfour\) and define
\[
\sigma(i)=i+1.
\]
Then \(\sigma^4=\id\), with no earlier positive return.

Declare \(i\) and \(j\) incident when
\[
j=\sigma(i)\quad\text{or}\quad j=\sigma^{-1}(i).
\]
This generates \(\Cfour\), with orientations supplied by
\(\sigma\) and \(\sigma^{-1}\).

Unit means one application of \(\sigma\). It does not mean the
identity. The visible order four is a premise, not a conclusion.
