\section{Binary lifts and generated incidence}

Let the receipt fiber be \(F=\Ztwo\) and put
\[
\widetilde V=V\times F.
\]
A deterministic fiber-covariant lift is forced to have the form
\[
T(i,s)=(i+1,s+v_i),
\qquad v_i\in\Ztwo.
\]
Define lifted incidence by one application of \(T\) or \(T^{-1}\).

\begin{definition}
The circuit holonomy is
\[
\omega=v_0+v_1+v_2+v_3\in\Ztwo.
\]
\end{definition}

Iteration gives the fundamental law
\[
T^4(i,s)=(i,s+\omega).
\]
Thus one visible circuit either preserves or exchanges the receipt
sheet.
