\section{Theorem boundary}

The exact implication is
\[
C_4+C_2+\text{nontrivial circuit holonomy}
\Longrightarrow C_8.
\]

The theorem does not derive the visible four-cycle, binary fiber, or
nontrivial receipt. Uniqueness is up to gauge and fiber-preserving
isomorphism, not edge labeling.

A quarter-turn representation may read four steps as \(360\) degrees
and eight as \(720\) degrees, but angles are not part of the proof.
No electron, gravity, radiation, force, field, energy, momentum, or
spacetime claim is made.
