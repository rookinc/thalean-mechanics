\section{Introduction}

A graph usually begins with specified vertices and edges. Here we
begin with a reversible successor, lift it through a binary receipt
fiber, and define incidence by one-step reachability.

Suppose a visible action returns after four steps while one complete
visible circuit changes a binary receipt. How many lifted steps are
required for complete return?

Every deterministic \(C_2\)-covariant two-sheeted lift of a
four-cycle belongs to one of two gauge classes. The trivial class is
two disjoint four-cycles. The nontrivial class is the eight-cycle.
This is elementary voltage-graph mathematics
\cite{gross-tucker,biggs}; the contribution here is its exact
organization as a finite action-receipt theorem.
