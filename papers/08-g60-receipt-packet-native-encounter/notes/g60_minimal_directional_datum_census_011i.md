## Stabilizer obstruction

For N, the stabilizer of one ordered duad has order 12 and fixes no root.

For either S5 complement, the stabilizer has order 6 and fixes no root.

An equivariant map is determined by the image of one basepoint, and that image
must be fixed by the source stabilizer. Since there is no fixed root, no map
can begin.

This is distinct from the earlier kernel obstruction. Removing the offending
kernel does not make the two twenty-point actions equivalent.

## Anchor boundary

An anchored root trivially names one local orientation. The failed statement
is stronger and global: that local choice cannot be propagated to all ordered
duads while respecting N or either complement.

Therefore the one-bit global-orientation description is not earned.
