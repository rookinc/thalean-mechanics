eight and order profile 1, 5, 2 in orders 1, 2, 4. Its inner
automorphism subgroup has order four and is V4, with order profile
1, 3. The nontrivial outer coset has four automorphisms: two of order
two and two of order four.

All four outer representatives are predicted to induce the same
nontrivial C2 exchange on the pair of chart-orbit classes. However,
only the two genuine order-two outer representatives are called outer
involutions.

The locked prediction that adjoining an outer involution gives an
extended action of order 960, one orbit of size 80, and stabilizer
order 12 is retained only for those two genuine outer involutions. No
extended-action order is preregistered here for the two order-four
