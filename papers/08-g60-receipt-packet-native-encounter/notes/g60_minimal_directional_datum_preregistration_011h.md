# G60 minimal directional datum preregistration 011h

## Question

Packet 011g proved that full native covariance selects inverse-root pairs but
cannot select individual roots. Packet 011h asks what smallest additional
datum removes that obstruction.

## Candidate ladder

The frozen ladder compares:

1. full Aut(G60);
2. the complete unordered family of S5 complements;
3. the kernel N of the action on that family;
4. one selected complement;
5. one anchored root above one named ordered duad.

The test must determine whether the complement choice contributes information
or whether its complete family already determines a canonical propagation
subgroup.

## Structural prediction

The non-blind prediction is that the two complements have one common
index-two normalizer N, that N admits exactly two inverse-related global
bridges, and that both complements admit the same two bridges. If so, choosing
one root above one already named duad selects one bridge, while deleting that
root choice restores two.

## Boundary

Any minimality statement is limited to this preregistered ladder. No global
information-theoretic minimality, physical direction, geometry, or physics
claim is allowed. The manuscript remains untouched.
