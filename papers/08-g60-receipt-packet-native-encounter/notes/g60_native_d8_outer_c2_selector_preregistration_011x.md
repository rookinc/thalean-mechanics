# G60 native D8 outer-C2 selector preregistration 011x

## Purpose

This packet preregisters a complete bounded automorphism census on the
locked 011w native D8 chart bundles. The test asks whether the residual
outer automorphism exchanges the two locked chart orbits and whether any
currently locked native marking canonically selects one of them.

No local D8 automorphism enumeration or outer-selector census has been
performed in this packet.

## Locked input

The 011w census established two full-A chart orbits of size forty for
each of the two gauge-related cocycle presentations. Each eight-chart
fiber splits into two inner-automorphism orbits of size four. The
alpha_1 character and q-axis signature are constant across both global
orbits. The four presentation-gauge maps preserve the two orbit classes.

## Preregistered prediction

The local D8 automorphism group has order eight, its inner subgroup has
order four, and its outer quotient is C2. Inner automorphisms preserve
each four-chart fiber orbit. Every outer automorphism exchanges those
fiber orbits and consequently exchanges the two global forty-chart
orbits.

After adjoining one outer involution, each eighty-chart presentation
bundle is predicted to become transitive. The two cocycle presentations
are predicted to carry equivalent outer-C2 torsors. Neither alpha_1 nor
the q-axis marking is predicted to distinguish the torsor values.

Therefore the bounded chart model is predicted to admit no selector
that is invariant under both the full native action and the residual
outer chart gauge. Selecting an absolute chart orbit requires one
additional two-valued torsor choice.

## Boundary

This is a preregistration, not a result. It does not prove that an
additional binary datum is necessary, select an outer orbit, construct
an absolute chart, construct a native update law, establish a mechanics
state cell, identify the local side with the 011o orientation sheet, or
make a geometry or physical claim.

The predicted necessity is bounded to the locked native D8 chart model.
It is not a global minimality claim over all possible constructions.
