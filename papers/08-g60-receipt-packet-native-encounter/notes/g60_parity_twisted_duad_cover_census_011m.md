# G60 parity-twisted duad cover census 011m

## Result

The preregistered parity-twisted double cover of the ten unordered
five-point duads was constructed and tested as an action of the canonical
normal subgroup `N` of order 240.

The source has twenty objects of the form

`(unordered_duad, epsilon)`,

where `epsilon` is a binary sheet coordinate. The five-point parity of an
element of `N` determines whether it preserves or exchanges the sheets.

The action is valid and transitive. Its single orbit has size 20.

Every source-object stabilizer has order 12 in `N`, has a five-point image
of order 6, and agrees exactly with the stabilizers of both roots in the
inverse pair assigned to the underlying unordered duad.

## Bridge census

Exactly two `N`-equivariant bijections from the twisted source to the
twenty orientation roots exist.

Their hashes are:

- `782dbcb9dae045cfc5dadd1b81f51895e447e981491263e09514c9077dfe1728`
- `bb61e5018270d8954e8115bb9c1fbbcfb07f360ba307c451cb16103ef7336c01`

Global source-sheet reversal exchanges the two maps. Root inversion also
exchanges the two maps, and these two operations agree on the bridge pair.

Each native `S5` complement admits exactly the same two maps. Complement
choice is therefore not load-bearing.

## Anchor ablation

There are forty compatible source-root anchors. Every compatible anchor
selects exactly one of the two bridges.

Without an anchor, both bridges remain valid.

Thus one binary sheet choice is sufficient within this preregistered
source action. This is a bounded sufficiency result, not a global
minimality theorem.

## Interpretation

The missing orientation representation was not ordinary endpoint ordering.
It is the parity-twisted double cover of unordered duads.

The earlier obstruction remains valid: the native unoriented datum does
not select one bridge. The new result identifies the exact two-sheeted
representation on which one additional anchored binary choice can
propagate consistently.

## Boundary

This packet constructs a replacement `N`-set, not a full
`Aut(G60)`-equivariant source set.

It does not select an orientation without an anchor, claim global
minimality, mutate the manuscript, derive geometry, or make a physical
claim.
