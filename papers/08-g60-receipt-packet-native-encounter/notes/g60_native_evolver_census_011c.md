# G60 native evolver census 011c

## Result

The complete exact census over the 480-element native automorphism group
returned

`U0_count = 0`.

There is no native automorphism H satisfying both

`order(H) = 8`

and

`H^4 = tau`,

where tau is the canonical nonidentity element of the center Z1.

Since U1 is a subset of U0 and U2 is a subset of U1, all three
preregistered families are empty.

The frozen classification is

`no_exact_evolver_candidate`.

## Meaning

This is a carrier obstruction, not yet an orientation obstruction.

The locked abstract algebra permits an order-eight evolver whose fourth
power is the binary receipt. That evolver is not realized as an element of
the native 60-vertex automorphism action under the preregistered bridge.

Therefore the conditional theorem about a symmetry exchanging H and
inverse(H) has not yet been instantiated natively. There is no H in the
declared carrier to reverse.

## Preserved negative boundary

U0 and U1 are not adopted as fallback families. They are empty anyway.
No replacement rule was searched, and no alternative fourth-power target
was chosen after inspection.

The result does not prove that no order-eight receipt evolver can exist on
a larger lifted carrier. It proves only that the exact declared evolver is
absent from Aut(G60) acting on the native 60 vertices.

A continuation must first define and preregister a larger carrier before
searching it. The likely mathematical question is whether the canonical
receipt tower constructs such a lift, not whether another element of the
same 480-element action can be retrofitted.

No manuscript, geometry, orientation, or physics claim is made.
