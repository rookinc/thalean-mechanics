\section{The native receipt-tower theorem}

We now combine the blind census, conditional normal-class selections,
exact unblindings, quotient factorization, voltage reconstructions, and
holonomy calculations.

\begin{theorem}[Native \(\Gsixty\) receipt tower]
Let \(\Gsixty\) be the registered native sixty-vertex graph, and assume
the exact full automorphism action used in the blind census.

\begin{enumerate}
\item If binary receipt and full-automorphism covariance are declared,
there is a unique admissible normal \(\Ctwo\) action.  Its quotient is
the preserved labeled graph \(\Gthirty\).

\item If \(\Vfour\) receipt and full-automorphism covariance are
declared, there is a unique admissible normal \(\Vfour\) action.  Its
quotient is the preserved labeled graph \(\Gfifteen\).

\item The recovered subgroups satisfy
\[
  \Ctwo\trianglelefteq\Vfour
  \trianglelefteq\Aut(\Gsixty),
\]
and their orbit maps form the exact tower
\[
  \Gsixty\longrightarrow\Gthirty\longrightarrow\Gfifteen.
\]

\item The native binary and \(\Vfour\) voltages each reconstruct
\(\Gsixty\) exactly.  Their holonomy images are respectively
\(\Ctwo\) and \(\Vfour\); hence both regular lifts are connected.

\item The recovered \(\Vfour\) voltage equals the independently
preserved certificate 033 voltage in the identity labeling, identity
basis, and zero gauge.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The blind census gives \(22\) admissible conjugacy classes and three
binary classes.  Its unique normal binary class is class \(22\).
Exact permutation, fiber, vertex-map, and quotient-edge comparison
identifies this class with the preserved \(\Gthirty\) deck cover.
The derived binary voltage reconstructs \(\Gsixty\), and its
fundamental receipts contain the nonidentity element of \(\Ctwo\).

The same census gives one normal \(\Vfour\), class \(20\), whose
members have registered indices \(0,65,124,326\).  Thus it contains
class \(22\).  Its fifteen four-point orbits produce the exact labeled
\(\Gfifteen\), while its induced quotient of the thirty binary fibers
agrees with the direct quotient.  The derived thirty-edge voltage
reconstructs the native edge set.  Its fundamental receipts span
\(\Ftwo^2\).  Finally, exhaustive graph-symmetry and basis comparison
contains the identity-label, identity-basis, zero-gauge match with
certificate 033.  Each claim is therefore exact.
\end{proof}

The keeper form is concise: the binary receipt finds \(\Gthirty\); the
\(\Vfour\) receipt finds \(\Gfifteen\); together they rebuild
\(\Gsixty\).
