\section{Boundary and conclusion}

The strongest temptation would be to say that the raw graph uniquely
contains the receipt tower.  The blind result forbids that statement.
There are \(22\) cover-admissible action classes.  The graph supplies a
spectrum of possible regular quotient structures.

What is proved is conditional and sharper.  Once binary receipt is
declared, full-automorphism covariance uniquely selects the historical
binary deck action.  Once \(\Vfour\) receipt is declared, the same
covariance principle uniquely selects the historical four-state
action.  These independently selected actions nest, their quotients
factor, their voltages reconstruct the source graph, and their
holonomies are surjective.

The result therefore closes the algebra-to-graph binding problem for
these declared receipt ranks.  The abstract packet did not merely fit
a previously labeled tower.  It first returned a nonunique native
spectrum, then recovered the old quotients through preregistered
conditional selectors, and finally reproduced the preserved voltage
data exactly.

No orientation is selected.  No physical backaction, spatial
extension, energy, electron, gravity, radiation, or quantum claim is
made.  Those interpretations would require additional premises and
independent theorems.

The mathematical conclusion is complete: native \(\Gsixty\) supports
an exact connected and reproducible
\[
  \Ctwo\leq\Vfour
\]
receipt tower over the preserved \(\Gthirty\) and \(\Gfifteen\)
quotients, conditional on declared receipt rank and covariance under
the full automorphism group.
