\section{Binary receipt recovery}

The blind census contains three conjugacy classes of semiregular
\(\Ctwo\) actions.  Their conjugacy-orbit sizes are \(15\), \(2\), and
\(1\).  Hence exactly one binary class is normal under the full
automorphism group.  It is blind class \(22\), represented in the
registered permutation action by element \(326\).

After this selection was frozen, the historical
\(\Gsixty\)-to-\(\Gthirty\) quotient certificate was opened.  The
selected permutation agrees with the preserved deck involution on all
sixty vertices, with zero disagreements.  Its thirty two-point orbits
equal the thirty preserved fibers.  The induced vertex map and all
sixty quotient edges agree exactly with the historical labeled
\(\Gthirty\).

\begin{proposition}[Normal binary recovery]
Conditional on binary receipt and covariance under
\(\Aut(\Gsixty)\), blind class \(22\) is the unique admissible action.
Its quotient is the exact preserved graph \(\Gthirty\).
\end{proposition}

Choose the minimum-labeled vertex in each binary fiber as sheet zero.
Every \(\Gthirty\) edge then receives a \(\Ctwo\) voltage.  Of the
sixty edge voltages, \(32\) are zero and \(28\) are one in this
section.  Rebuilding the regular lift produces exactly sixty vertices
and exactly the \(120\) native \(\Gsixty\) edges, with no missing or
extra edge.

The connected graph \(\Gthirty\) has cycle rank
\[
  60-30+1=31.
\]

contain eleven zeros and twenty ones.  The image is therefore all of
\(\Ctwo\), proving connectedness independently through the receipt
classification theorem.
