\section{The four-phase corollary}

Set (n=4). A visible four-phase successor has order four. Every deterministic
binary lift belongs to one of two classes:
\[
C_4\sqcup C_4
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
C_8.
\]
If one visible four-step circuit exchanges the receipt sheet, then
\[
H^4=\deck\neq1,
\qquad
H^8=1,
\]
and the connected (C_8) lift is forced uniquely up to gauge.

This recovers the order-eight forcing theorem of the finite four-phase
apparatus. The present result shows that eight is not selected independently
of four. It is the universal doubled order (2n) evaluated at (n=4).

The same conclusion holds at every finite cycle length. For example, a
nontrivial binary receipt over (C_3) forces (C_6), while one over (C_5)
forces (C_{10}). The mechanism is receipt holonomy, not the special geometry
of a square.
