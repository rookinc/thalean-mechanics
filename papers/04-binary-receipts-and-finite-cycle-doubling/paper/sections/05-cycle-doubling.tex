\section{Cycle doubling and retained return}

The nontrivial class has a useful exact-sequence description:
\[
1\longrightarrow\langle\deck\rangle\cong C_2
\longrightarrow C_{2n}
\overset{\pi}{\longrightarrow}C_n
\longrightarrow1.
\]
If (H) denotes the lifted successor, then
\[
|H|=2n,
\qquad
H^n=\deck,
\qquad
\deck^2=1.
\]
The visible projection has already returned after (n) steps, but the lifted
state has not. The deck element is therefore an exact finite receipt of the
completed visible circuit.

\begin{corollary}[Order forcing]
Suppose a deterministic binary lift of a visible order-(n) successor has a
nontrivial full-circuit receipt. Then its lifted successor has order exactly
(2n), and the lifted cycle is uniquely (C_{2n}) up to fiber-preserving
gauge and isomorphism.
\end{corollary}

The qualifier ``nontrivial'' is load bearing. A binary fiber alone does not
force connectedness or doubling. It supplies two possible lift classes. The
observed sheet exchange after one full circuit selects the connected class.
