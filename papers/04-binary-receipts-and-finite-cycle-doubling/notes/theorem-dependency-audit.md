# Theorem dependency audit

The classification requires exactly these premises:

1. a directed visible cycle of length `n >= 3`;
2. a two-element fiber over every visible vertex;
3. a deterministic lifted successor covering the visible successor;
4. covariance under the common deck flip;
5. fiber-preserving gauge equivalence.

These premises reduce every lifted edge map to addition of one voltage
`v_i` in `C_2`. Gauge transformations alter the local voltages by a binary
coboundary and preserve their total sum. Conversely, voltage assignments with
the same total sum differ by such a coboundary. The total sum is therefore a
complete invariant.

No connectedness assumption is needed. Connectedness is equivalent to
nontrivial holonomy and is a conclusion of the nontrivial-receipt branch.
