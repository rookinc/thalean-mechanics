# Source ledger

The proof in this paper is self-contained. It uses only elementary finite
group actions, binary voltage assignments, and explicit gauge transformations.

Paper 1, `01-unit-action-and-finite-geometric-emergence`, is historical
context and becomes the `n=4` corollary. Paper 3,
`03-encounter-orientation-receipt-tower`, uses that special case inside a
larger receipt tower. Neither paper is required as a premise of the present
proof.

The executable audit independently enumerates every voltage assignment for
cycle sizes 3 through 12. The enumeration is a finite verification witness,
not a substitute for the symbolic proof for arbitrary `n`.
