\section{Complete gauge classification}

\begin{theorem}[Holonomy classification]
Two deterministic regular $R$-covariant lifts of the directed cycle $C_n$
are equivariantly gauge equivalent if and only if their circuit holonomies
are conjugate in $R$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Gauge-equivalent lifts have conjugate holonomies by the telescoping identity.
Conversely, normalize the two voltage assignments to
$(e,\ldots,e,h)$ and $(e,\ldots,e,k)$. If
\[
k=t^{-1}ht,
\]
then the constant gauge $g_i=t$ on every fiber takes the first normalized
assignment to the second. Composing the normalizing gauges proves the result.
\end{proof}

Hence the classification set is
\[
\{\text{regular deterministic lifts of }C_n\}/\text{gauge}
\cong
\{\text{conjugacy classes of }R\}.
\]

This statement distinguishes two kinds of information. The conjugacy class
classifies the equivariant cover. The order of a representative determines
its cycle profile. Distinct conjugacy classes can have the same element order
and therefore the same profile without being gauge equivalent.
