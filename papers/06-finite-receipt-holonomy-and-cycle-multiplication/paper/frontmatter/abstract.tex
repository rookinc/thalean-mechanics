\begin{abstract}
Let $C_n$ be a directed visible cycle and let a finite group $R$ act
regularly on a receipt fiber over each visible state. We classify every
deterministic lift of the visible successor that is covariant under this
regular action. Such a lift is specified by edge voltages
$v_0,\ldots,v_{n-1}\in R$. Their ordered product
\[
h=v_0v_1\cdots v_{n-1}
\]
is the full-circuit receipt. Fiber-preserving equivariant gauge transformation
conjugates $h$, and every lift is gauge equivalent to the one-edge normal
form $(e,\ldots,e,h)$. Consequently, gauge-equivalence classes are in
bijection with conjugacy classes of circuit holonomy in $R$.

If $h$ has order $d$, then the lifted successor has $|R|/d$ orbits, each of
length $nd$. Hence the lift is connected exactly when $d=|R|$, equivalently
when $R$ is cyclic and $h$ generates it. For a cyclic receipt group $C_m$
with generating holonomy, the unique connected regular lift is $C_{nm}$.
Binary cycle doubling is the case $R=C_2$ and $d=2$. Thus doubling is one
instance of a general cycle-multiplication law: lifted cycle length equals
visible cycle length times the order of the retained circuit receipt. No
physical realization is claimed.
\end{abstract}
