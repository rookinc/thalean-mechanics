# Source ledger

The proof is self-contained. It uses finite groups, regular group actions,
directed cycle lifts, and elementary gauge normalization.

Paper 4 is recovered as the binary corollary `R=C2`. Paper 5 studies an
additional involutive factorization after that binary lift has been formed.
Those papers are context rather than premises of the present theorem.

The executable audit exhausts selected cycle lengths over the receipt groups
`C2`, `C3`, `C4`, `C5`, `C6`, `V4`, `S3`, and the dihedral group of order
eight. Both abelian and nonabelian gauge behavior are tested. The audit is a
finite implementation witness; the arbitrary finite-group theorem is proved
symbolically in the manuscript.
