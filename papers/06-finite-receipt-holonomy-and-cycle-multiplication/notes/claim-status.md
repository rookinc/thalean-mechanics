# Claim status

Closed:

- every deterministic regular `R`-covariant lift of `C_n` has a unique
  edge-voltage description;
- circuit holonomy is the ordered product of the edge voltages;
- gauge transformation conjugates circuit holonomy;
- every lift is gauge equivalent to a one-edge normal form;
- two lifts are gauge equivalent exactly when their holonomies are conjugate;
- if holonomy has order `d`, the lift has `|R|/d` components;
- every component has length `n*d`;
- the lift is connected exactly when the holonomy generates the entire
  receipt group;
- connectedness therefore forces the receipt group to be cyclic;
- for `R=C_m` with generating holonomy, `C_n` lifts to `C_nm`;
- binary cycle doubling is the special case `R=C2`.

Open or intentionally excluded:

- derivation of a receipt group from native or physical structure;
- selection of a particular holonomy conjugacy class;
- interpretation of receipt order as physical duration, energy, or distance;
- all force, field, gravity, radiation, particle, and spacetime claims.
