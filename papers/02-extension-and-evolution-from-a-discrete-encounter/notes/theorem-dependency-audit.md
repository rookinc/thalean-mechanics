# Theorem dependency audit

General premises:

1. A group Gamma with a quotient q to an abelian encounter algebra E.
2. Lifted action and carrier elements A and B.

General consequences:

1. q(AB)=q(BA).
2. [A,B] belongs to ker(q).
3. AB=[A,B]BA.

Additional involutive premise:

A^2=B^2=1.

Consequences:

H=AB, H inverse=BA, and [A,B]=H^2.

The order-eight evolver and D16 group belong to the finite realization,
not to the unrestricted general theorem.
