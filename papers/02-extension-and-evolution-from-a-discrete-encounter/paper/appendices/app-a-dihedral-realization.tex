\section{Finite realization census}

On the eight points \(\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}\), define
\[
A(k)=-k,
\qquad
B(k)=-k-1.
\]
Then
\[
AB(k)=k+1,
\qquad
BA(k)=k-1.
\]
Thus \(H=AB\) is the positive successor and \(BA=H^{-1}\) is the
negative successor.

\begin{tabular}{c|c}
Quantity & Value\\
\hline
Group order & 16\\
Order of A and B & 2\\
Order of H & 8\\
Visible quotient & \(C_2\times C_2\)\\
Kernel & \(\langle H^2\rangle\), order 4\\
Commutator & \(H^2\)\\
\(H\)-orbit & \(C_8\)
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
