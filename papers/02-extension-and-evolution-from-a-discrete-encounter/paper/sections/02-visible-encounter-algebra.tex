\section{The visible encounter algebra}

Let
\[
E=\{1,a,b,ab\}\cong C_2\times C_2,
\]
with \(a^2=b^2=1\) and \(ab=ba\). We read \(1\) as the unregistered
state, \(a\) and \(b\) as two registered roles, and \(ab\) as their visible
encounter.

This interpretation adds no physical content. Algebraically, the key
fact is simply that \(E\) is abelian. It therefore cannot distinguish
\(ab\) from \(ba\). A lifted algebra is required if encounter order is to remain
available.
