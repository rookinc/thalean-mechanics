\section{A finite dihedral realization}

Let
\[
\Gamma_8=
\langle A,B\mid A^2=B^2=1,(AB)^8=1\rangle.
\]
We use \(D_{16}\) for the dihedral group of order sixteen. Put \(H=AB\). Then \(H\)
has order eight and every element has one of the normal forms
\[
H^k\quad\text{or}\quad AH^k,
\qquad 0\leq k<8.
\]

The map \(q(A)=a\) and \(q(B)=b\) defines a surjection to \(E\). Its kernel is
\[
\Ker(q)=\langle H^2\rangle
=\{1,H^2,H^4,H^6\}\cong C_4.
\]
The commutator \(H^2\) is therefore a nontrivial kernel element.

Conjugation reverses the evolver:
\[
AHA=H^{-1},
\qquad
BHB=H^{-1}.
\]
The \(H\)-orbit is \(C_8\). Its cyclic orientation quotient
\(C_8\to C_4\) has kernel \(\{1,H^4\}\), so \(H^4\) returns visibly
while remaining nontrivial
upstairs.
