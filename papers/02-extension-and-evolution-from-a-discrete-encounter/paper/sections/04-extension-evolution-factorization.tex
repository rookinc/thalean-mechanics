\section{Extension and evolution}

The quotient defines an exact sequence
\[
1\longrightarrow K\longrightarrow\Gamma
\overset{q}{\longrightarrow}E\longrightarrow1,
\qquad K=\Ker(q).
\]

The image \(q(w)\) records the visible state of a lifted word \(w\).
Kernel information records distinctions erased by \(q\). After choosing
a set-theoretic section \(s:E\to\Gamma\), one may write
\[
w=k(w)s(q(w)),
\qquad k(w)\in K.
\]
This general factorization depends on s and is not canonical.

The commutator \([A,B]\) is canonical relative to \(A\) and \(B\). It
requires no section and measures the specific distinction between
\(AB\) and \(BA\).
This motivates the terminology extension receipt.

The balance is multiplicative and informational, not a numerical
conservation law.
