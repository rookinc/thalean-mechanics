\section{The lifted encounter}

Let \(\Gamma\) be a group with a surjection \(q:\Gamma\to E\). Choose
lifted elements \(A\) and \(B\) satisfying \(q(A)=a\) and \(q(B)=b\). Define
\[
H=AB.
\]

\begin{proposition}
The ordered products \(AB\) and \(BA\) have the same visible image, and
\[
[A,B]\in\Ker(q),
\qquad
AB=[A,B]BA.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Since \(E\) is abelian,
\[
q(AB)=ab=ba=q(BA).
\]
Moreover,
\[
q([A,B])=aba^{-1}b^{-1}=1,
\]
so the commutator lies in the kernel. Finally,
\[
[A,B]BA=ABA^{-1}B^{-1}BA=AB.
\]
\end{proof}

If \(A\) and \(B\) are involutions, then
\[
H^{-1}=(AB)^{-1}=BA
\]
and
\[
[A,B]=ABAB=H^2.
\]
