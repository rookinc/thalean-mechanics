\section{Theorem boundary}

The general theorem proves only that an abelian visible quotient
forgets encounter order and that the corresponding commutator lies in
the kernel. Involutivity gives \(H^{-1}=BA\) and \([A,B]=H^2\).

Order eight, the order-sixteen dihedral group, and the explicit kernel
belong to the finite realization. They are not forced by the general
commutator identity alone.

Extension means enlargement of the algebraic state space required to
retain order information. It does not mean physical spatial
expansion. Backaction is a possible later interpretation, not a
theorem conclusion. No force, energy, electron, gravity, radiation,
field, momentum, or spacetime claim is made.
