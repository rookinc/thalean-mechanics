\section{Introduction}

A commutative state algebra can record which roles participate in an
encounter while forgetting the order in which they act. This paper
isolates the missing information as a commutator residue in the kernel
of a lifted quotient.

The main distinction is structural:
\[
\text{evolution}=\text{quotient-visible encounter},
\qquad
\text{extension}=\text{kernel-valued retained order}.
\]
The underlying group identities are standard
\cite{dummit-foote,rotman}. The contribution is their bounded
organization as a finite action-receipt construction.
