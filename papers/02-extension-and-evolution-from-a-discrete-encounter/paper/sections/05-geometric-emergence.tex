\section{Geometry generated by the evolver}

Consider the orbit
\[
1,H,H^2,H^3,\ldots .
\]
Declare two orbit states incident when one application of \(H\) or
\(H^{-1}\) connects them. If \(H\) has finite order \(n\), this generated
incidence graph is \(C_n\). The choices \(H\) and \(H^{-1}\) supply its two
orientations.

Thus action \(A\) and carrier \(B\) produce an ordered evolver \(H=AB\);
iteration of \(H\) produces states; one-step reachability produces incidence.
Geometry is generated from the encounter orbit rather than supplied
as an independent background.
