# Claim status

## Proved abstractly

- Every deterministic regular finite-group lift of a connected finite graph
  is described by inverse-compatible edge receipts.
- Vertex gauge changes conjugate based path holonomy.
- A spanning-tree gauge removes every tree-edge receipt.
- Gauge classes are classified by conjugacy classes of representations of the
  graph fundamental group in the receipt group.
- If `H` is the image of the holonomy representation, the lifted graph has
  index `[R:H]` connected components.
- Each component contains `|V(X)| |H|` lifted vertices.
- The lift is connected exactly when `H=R`.
- The single-cycle theorem is recovered by `H=<h>`.

## Computationally certified

The executable audit exhausts the declared voltage assignments on trees,
cycles, theta graphs, and a two-cycle bouquet over cyclic, noncyclic abelian,
and nonabelian receipt groups.

## Not claimed

- No receipt group is derived from a physical graph.
- No native edge receipt is selected.
- No absolute orientation is selected.
- No G60, G900, G15, electron, gravity, emission, time, energy, or force claim
  is made.
- No claim of novelty over voltage-graph and regular-cover theory is made.
