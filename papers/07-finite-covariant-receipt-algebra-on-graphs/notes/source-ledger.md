# Source ledger

## Mathematical lineage

The manuscript uses standard ideas from voltage graphs, regular graph covers,
spanning-tree normalization, and graph fundamental groups. The primary
reference is Gross and Tucker, with Serre cited for the tree and fundamental
group viewpoint.

## Internal theorem lineage

Paper 6 established the complete classification for a single visible cycle.
Paper 7 replaces that one-generator fundamental group with the free
fundamental group of an arbitrary connected finite graph.

## Independent certificate boundary

The abstract theorem does not provide data for a raw graph. A raw application
must independently supply and hash-lock:

1. base graph;
2. lifted graph or fiber projection;
3. finite receipt group and multiplication table;
4. regular fiber action;
5. oriented edge receipts;
6. inverse-edge compatibility;
7. lift reconstruction isomorphism;
8. gauge- and spanning-tree-independent holonomy data.
