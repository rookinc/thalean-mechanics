\section{The holonomy subgroup and connected components}

Define the holonomy subgroup
\[
H_{\alpha}=\im(\rho_{\alpha})\leq R.
\]
It consists exactly of receipts of closed walks based at $r$.

\begin{theorem}[Component theorem]
The lifted graph $\widetilde X_{\alpha}$ has exactly
\[
[R:H_{\alpha}]=\frac{|R|}{|H_{\alpha}|}
\]
connected components. Every component contains
\[
|V(X)|\,|H_{\alpha}|
\]
vertices. In particular,
\[
\widetilde X_{\alpha}\text{ is connected}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
H_{\alpha}=R.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
At the root fiber, a lifted closed walk sends $(r,x)$ to $(r,xh)$ for some
$h\in H_{\alpha}$. Conversely, every $h\in H_{\alpha}$ is realized by a
based closed walk. Thus the root-fiber vertices reachable from $(r,x)$ are
exactly
\[
\{(r,xh):h\in H_{\alpha}\}.
\]
They are indexed by the left coset $xH_{\alpha}$.

For any base vertex $v$, choose a path $P_v$ from $r$ to $v$. The vertices
over $v$ in the same component are
\[
\{(v,xh\alpha(P_v)):h\in H_{\alpha}\},
\]
again a set of size $|H_{\alpha}|$. Hence each component contains
$|V(X)|\,|H_{\alpha}|$ vertices, and the components are indexed by the
left cosets of $H_{\alpha}$ in $R$.
\end{proof}

The conjugacy class of $H_{\alpha}$ is gauge invariant. Its order, index, and
the resulting component census are therefore intrinsic to the gauge class.
