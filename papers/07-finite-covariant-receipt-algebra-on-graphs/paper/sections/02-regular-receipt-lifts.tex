\section{Regular receipt lifts}

Let $X=(V,E)$ be a finite connected graph. Write $D(X)$ for its oriented
edges, or darts. Each $a\in D(X)$ has a tail $t(a)$, a head $h(a)$, and an
opposite dart $\bar a$ satisfying
\[
t(\bar a)=h(a),\qquad h(\bar a)=t(a),\qquad \bar{\bar a}=a.
\]
Let $R$ be a finite group with identity $e$.

\begin{definition}[Receipt assignment]
An inverse-compatible receipt assignment is a map
\[
\alpha:D(X)\longrightarrow R
\]
satisfying
\[
\alpha(\bar a)=\alpha(a)^{-1}
\]
for every dart $a$.
\end{definition}

The associated lifted graph $\widetilde X_{\alpha}$ has vertex set
\[
V(\widetilde X_{\alpha})=V(X)\times R.
\]
For every dart $a:u\to v$ and every $x\in R$, it has a lifted dart
\[
(a,x):(u,x)\longrightarrow (v,x\alpha(a)).
\]
Inverse compatibility ensures that $(\bar a,x\alpha(a))$ reverses this lifted
dart.

The group $R$ acts on the lift by left multiplication:
\[
L_r(v,x)=(v,rx).
\]
Because left multiplication commutes with right multiplication, every $L_r$
is a deck transformation of the projection
\[
p:\widetilde X_{\alpha}\longrightarrow X,\qquad p(v,x)=v.
\]

Conversely, after one point in each regular $R$-fiber has been chosen, every
regular $R$-covariant graph lift has such a receipt description. Changing
those chosen fiber origins is precisely gauge.
