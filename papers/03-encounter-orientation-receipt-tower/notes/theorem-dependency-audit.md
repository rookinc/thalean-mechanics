# Theorem dependency audit

The bridge theorem requires four explicit premises:

1. a visible cyclic four-state orientation system;
2. a deterministic C2-covariant two-sheet lift;
3. nontrivial full-circuit binary holonomy;
4. a factorization H=AB by two involutions.

Premises 1-3 force the connected C8 lift. Premise 4 then forces the
D16 reversal completion. The encounter quotient E is induced by
mapping A and B to the two generators of V4.

The proof never identifies E with O. Their relation is mediated by the
lifted elements H, c=H^2, and tau=H^4.
