\section{Dihedral completion}

Suppose the forced order-eight successor factors as \(H=AB\), where
\(A^2=B^2=1\). Then
\[
AHA=H^{-1}.
\]
Every word in \(A\) and \(B\) reduces to one of the normal forms
\[
H^k
\quad\text{or}\quad
AH^k,
\qquad 0\leq k<8.
\]
These sixteen forms are distinct, so
\[
\langle A,B\rangle\cong D_{16}.
\]

The encounter quotient
\[
q_E:D_{16}\longrightarrow V_4
\]
has kernel
\[
\Ker(q_E)=\langle H^2\rangle
=\{1,H^2,H^4,H^6\}\cong C_4.
\]
The orientation projection on \(\langle H\rangle\) has kernel
\[
\Ker(p_O)=\{1,H^4\}=\{1,\tau\}\cong C_2.
\]

Thus the dihedral completion contains both four-state quotients
without identifying them. Their compatibility is mediated by the
receipt tower inside the lifted group.
