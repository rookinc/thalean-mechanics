\section{The lifted encounter}

Let \(A\) and \(B\) be involutions and put
\[
H=AB.
\]
Then
\[
H^{-1}=(AB)^{-1}=BA.
\]
The two orders of encounter therefore define inverse evolvers.

Set
\[
c=[A,B]=ABA^{-1}B^{-1}.
\]
Because \(A^{-1}=A\) and \(B^{-1}=B\),
\[
c=ABAB=H^2,
\qquad
AB=cBA.
\]

Now define the encounter quotient by
\[
q_E(A)=a,\qquad q_E(B)=b.
\]
Since \(E\) is abelian,
\[
q_E(H)=ab,\qquad q_E(c)=1.
\]
Thus the visible encounter sees the common product class but erases
the commutator that distinguishes \(AB\) from \(BA\).
