\section{The receipt tower}

Assume \(H\) has order eight. Restrict attention to the cyclic evolver
subgroup \(\langle H\rangle\cong C_8\). Define the orientation
projection
\[
p_O:\langle H\rangle\longrightarrow O,
\qquad
p_O(H)=r.
\]

Let
\[
c=H^2,
\qquad
\tau=c^2=H^4.
\]
The two quotient readouts are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{lifted element} & q_E & p_O\\
\hline
H & ab & r\\
c=H^2 & 1 & r^2\\
\tau=H^4 & 1 & 1
\end{array}
\]

The extension receipt \(c\) is invisible to the encounter algebra but
visible as the orientation half-turn. The binary receipt \(\tau\) is
invisible to both visible readouts while remaining nonidentity in the
lift.

The subgroup tower is
\[
\langle\tau\rangle\cong C_2
\;\triangleleft\;
\langle c\rangle\cong C_4
\;\triangleleft\;
\langle H\rangle\cong C_8.
\]
In particular, the binary return receipt is not an unrelated added
label. It is the square of the encounter commutator.
