\section{Introduction}

Two four-state systems appear naturally in the preceding finite
action constructions. One registers the roles in an encounter. The
other registers the phases of a directed cycle. Equal cardinality does
not make them the same algebra.

This paper proves the missing compatibility theorem. The encounter
algebra is \(V_4\), the orientation algebra is \(C_4\), and their
relationship exists only in a larger lifted apparatus. The elements
\[
H,\qquad c=H^2,\qquad \tau=H^4
\]
form the bridge. They represent, respectively, directed evolution, an
order-sensitive extension receipt, and a hidden binary return receipt.

The proof combines an exhaustive two-sheet lift classification with a
finite dihedral completion. The underlying group facts are standard
\cite{dummit-foote,rotman}; the contribution is their exact assembly
as a receipt tower with explicit nonidentification and theorem
boundaries.
