\section{Boundary and conclusion}

The theorem is conditional. It does not derive the visible four-cycle,
binary fiber, nontrivial holonomy, or involutive factorization from a
physical system. It proves what these premises force once supplied.

The words action, encounter, evolution, extension, and receipt name
algebraic roles. Extension means kernel-valued retained distinction,
not spatial expansion. The theorem contains no force, field, time,
energy, electron, gravity, radiation, momentum, or spacetime claim.

Within that boundary, the result closes the abstract apparatus. The
encounter quotient records participation. The orientation quotient
records cyclic phase. The commutator retains order. Its square retains
the completed return. Four visible phases and one nontrivial binary
receipt force the eight-state lift; involutive encounter roles force
its dihedral completion.
