\section{Finite census}

On \(\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}\), define
\[
A(k)=-k,
\qquad
B(k)=-k-1.
\]
Then
\[
AB(k)=k+1,
\qquad
BA(k)=k-1.
\]

Extension receipt & \([A,B]=H^2\)\\
Binary receipt & \([A,B]^2=H^4\)
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The accompanying audit enumerates all sixteen binary voltage
assignments on the visible four-cycle. Eight have trivial holonomy and
cycle profile \((4,4)\); eight have nontrivial holonomy and cycle
profile \((8)\). It independently verifies the two quotient maps,
their kernels, the commutator identities, and all sixteen elements of
the dihedral completion.
