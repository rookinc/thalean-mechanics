# Canonical G60 Representation

Version: 0.1.0

## Purpose

This document establishes the canonical mathematical representation of the
canonical thalion.

It does not define the ontology of the thalion.

It defines the chosen computational representation used throughout
Thalean Mechanics.

---

## Statement

The Thalean Graph (G60) is the chosen canonical computational representation
of the canonical thalion.

The canonical thalion is defined by the ontology of Thalean Mechanics.

The Thalean Graph (G60) provides the canonical mathematical scaffold for the canonical computational representation of the canonical thalion.

## Purpose of the Representation

The canonical G60 representation provides:

- the canonical sixty-address space
- canonical neighborhood relationships
- canonical locality
- canonical symmetry
- canonical computational scaffold

These properties belong to the representation.

The ontology belongs to the thalion.

---

## Boundary

The canonical thalion is not the Thalean Graph.

The Thalean Graph is the chosen canonical computational representation of the
canonical thalion.

Future computational representations may exist.

Such representations extend Thalean Mechanics without redefining the ontology
of the thalion.

---

## Research Boundary

Thalean Mechanics develops:

- ontology
- mechanics
- laws

Graph Theory develops:

- representations

- mathematical scaffolds

The current canonical mathematical scaffold is the Thalean Graph (G60).
